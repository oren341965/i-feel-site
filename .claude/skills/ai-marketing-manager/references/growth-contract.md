# Qualified-lead growth contract

Use this contract for an I Feel marketing plan, weekly growth review, experiment backlog or channel decision.

## Decision gates

Do not recommend scaling until all four gates are known:

1. **Evidence freshness:** state the source window and whether every required page or result set was read.
2. **Tracking trust:** distinguish platform conversions from verified leads and document unresolved tracking gaps.
3. **Attribution confidence:** connect source to qualified lead, proposal, win and revenue through stable identifiers.
4. **Sales capacity:** confirm the team can respond within the current operating threshold. When the threshold or capacity evidence is missing, scaling is blocked.

## Metrics

Primary decision metrics are qualified leads, cost per qualified lead, proposal rate, win rate and attributed revenue. Raw leads, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, video views and landing-page conversion are diagnostic metrics only.

Use comparable windows and labels. Prefer 7-day operational checks, 30-day channel decisions and 90-day directional context when the data supports them. Never present a forecast without its assumptions and uncertainty.

## Experiment record

Every proposed experiment must include:

- stable experiment key and owner;
- diagnosed constraint and supporting evidence;
- channel, audience, offer and destination;
- one primary decision metric and at least one guardrail;
- baseline, target or minimum detectable signal when available;
- start/end window and stopping rule;
- expected platform, publishing, send or budget mutation;
- approval state: `NOW`, `NEEDS_APPROVAL`, `BLOCKED` or `WATCH`.

Rank experiments directionally by expected qualified-pipeline impact, confidence and effort. Do not hide a weak evidence gate behind a numerical score.

## Weekly handoff

Lead with the current bottleneck and the single best next experiment. Then report channel evidence, qualification and downstream funnel movement, capacity, tracking or attribution gaps, approvals required and the next review date. Separate observed results from hypotheses.
