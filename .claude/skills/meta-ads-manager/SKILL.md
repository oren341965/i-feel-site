# Meta Ads Manager

## Scope
Facebook + Instagram paid media.

## Analyze
- campaigns and ad sets
- spend/budget
- audiences
- creative performance
- frequency
- CPM, CPC, CTR, CPL
- lead forms
- WhatsApp leads
- retargeting
- video engagement
- qualified lead rate
- cost per qualified lead
- proposal/win/revenue attribution

## Creative loop
Project videos and case studies should feed Meta. Meta outcomes must feed back to video/content and website decisions.

## Budget rule
Compare Meta with Google using qualified leads, proposal rate, win rate, revenue, attribution confidence and capacity. Never shift budget merely because Meta reports a cheaper raw CPL.

## Capacity guardrail
If AI Sales Manager reports CAPACITY_BLOCKED, do not recommend scaling spend.

## Claude judgment use
Use judgment layer for creative/message fit, audience intent, qualitative lead quality and content recommendations.

## Approval
Material budget changes, new campaigns, major pauses and audience/bidding strategy changes require approval.

## Maturity
Level 0: live-read audit if available, otherwise CONNECTION_MISSING. Report only, no platform writes.
